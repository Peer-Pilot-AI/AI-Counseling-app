Privacy Notice

Version 2026-09-27 · Local beta

This notice describes the current local build. It is not a compliance certification or legal advice. Have qualified counsel review privacy, student-record, and child-safety requirements before real-student or public deployment.

Information the app stores

The app stores your account email and a one-way password hash, plus information you choose to provide: name, grade, school and location, optional GPA, interests, goals, courses, activities, awards, work and volunteer experience, target colleges, tasks, saved items, application records, essays, conversations, and feedback. The app does not ask for your birth date; account creation uses age and, when applicable, guardian-permission confirmations.

How information is used

Account information is used to sign you in and keep your profile and planning workspace private to your account. AI requests may include the question, recent conversation turns, and relevant profile details. Essay-feedback requests include the essay draft and prompt you submit. The app uses these details to personalize its response. Do not submit information you would not want processed for this purpose.

AI, web research, and memory

When configured, the Python server sends AI requests to Google's Gemini API. Requests can include your question, recent conversation turns, upcoming deadlines, relevant profile details, and your saved memory notes. Essay-feedback requests include the draft and prompt you submit. For research questions the server may search the web (DuckDuckGo) and open public web pages on your behalf; the search words and page addresses leave the server, never your account details. The app stores the answer, source links, retrieval time, request category, and response time in your account's research history. Google's handling of data depends on the API plan the operator chose (free-tier prompts may be used to improve Google's products) and is governed by Google's terms; this notice does not make claims about provider retention.

Memory: your AI coach can save short notes about your goals and preferences so it can help you better. Notes belong to your account only. You can see, edit, delete, or switch off memory on the “What AI remembers” page, and deleting your account deletes them. The AI is told not to save passwords, contact information, or private details such as health information.

Where data is stored and who can access it

In the local build, SQLite stores account data on the computer running the Python server. The app does not publish student profiles. A configured administrator can access account-management and quality-report tools; AI failure reports may include a question and error details. Protect the computer, database file, backups, environment file, and administrator credentials. Do not expose the local server publicly without security and privacy review.

Cookies, reset, and deletion

The app uses a random, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie to keep you signed in. Password reset is a local development flow: a reset code may be shown by the app and no email delivery is configured. You may sign out or request deletion in your profile. Account deletion removes account-linked data through database relationships; maintainers should also securely handle backups and any records already held by service providers.

Minors and parent or guardian permission

Users must confirm they are at least 13. Users under 18 must have a parent or guardian review the Terms of Service and this Privacy Notice and give permission before account creation. The checkbox is a user confirmation, not an independent identity or age-verification system.

Questions and future changes

This local beta has no support contact address. Before deployment, add an operator identity, privacy contact, retention periods, applicable rights and request process, incident procedure, and region-specific notices. Material changes should receive legal review and be communicated to users.
